# README
Your website is built as a single, streamlined page that contains all key information in clearly defined sections. Instead of navigating between multiple pages, visitors move smoothly through the site using the fixed sidebar menu.
This approach keeps the site fast, simple to maintain, and easy for users to understand.

## Navigation & Layout
The sidebar on the left contains links such as Home, Why Us, Services, Projects, Reviews, and Contact.
Each of these links scrolls the visitor directly to the relevant section on the page. There are no page reloads, everything happens instantly, which improves usability and performance.

## Page Sections Explained
### Hero (Top of Page)
This is the first thing visitors see. It clearly communicates:
Who you are
What you do
Who you serve (Melbourne residential & light commercial)
It’s designed to immediately set expectations and build trust.

### Why Choose Us
This section highlights your key strengths:
Custom system design
High-quality installation
Customer-focused approach
It helps visitors quickly understand why they should choose you over competitors.

### Services
Each service is presented in its own card with:
A visual image
A short overview
Key benefits
A clear highlight
Services include ducted systems, split systems, hydronic heating, underfloor heating, new builds, and servicing. New services can be added easily by duplicating one of these cards.

### Contracting
This section explains your offering for builders, integrators, and high-end projects. It positions Degree Celsius as a trusted technical partner rather than just an installer.

### Featured Project
A project video is embedded directly into the page. It uses a preview image (thumbnail) so the site loads quickly and only plays when the user clicks.
This section is designed to visually demonstrate workmanship and quality.

### Client Testimonials
Customer reviews are displayed in clean, easy-to-read cards. Each testimonial focuses on a specific type of work, making it relatable for new visitors.
Additional testimonials can be added at any time without changing the site structure.

### Contact Form
The enquiry form sends messages directly to your inbox using a secure third-party service (Formspree).
When someone submits the form:
Their details are securely captured
The enquiry is emailed to you
No website login or backend server is required
Basic spam protection is included, and the form works reliably across desktop and mobile.

## Design & Styling
The overall look and feel of the site (colours, spacing, layout, cards, grids) is controlled by a central stylesheet. This ensures the design stays consistent across the entire site and can be updated easily without touching the content.

## Images & Media
All images and videos are stored locally within the website’s asset folders. Updating photos or replacing the project video is as simple as swapping out the file, no redesign required.

## SEO & Google Visibility
The site is built with search engines in mind:
Clear page titles and descriptions
Structured business data that tells Google what services you offer and where you operate
Proper indexing and canonical URLs
This helps Google understand the business and improves how the site appears in search results over time.

## Ongoing Updates
You can:
Edit text content
Add or remove services
Add more testimonials
Replace images or videos
All without needing to rebuild the site.
